# The official home of RFCs

RFCs outline computer networking and Internet foundations, including [Internet Standards](https://www.rfc-editor.org/search/?status=Internet+Standard) and historical or informative content. They are published by the RFC Editor for the [IETF](https://www.ietf.org), [IRTF](https://www.irtf.org), [IAB](https://www.iab.org), and [ISE](https://www.rfc-editor.org/authors/rfc-independent-submissions/), which collectively form the authoritative source for RFCs.
