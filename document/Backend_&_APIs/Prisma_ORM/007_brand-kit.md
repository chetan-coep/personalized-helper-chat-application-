Clear space & sizing

## Give the mark room to breathe

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the prism symbol (1×). Nothing — type, edges, other logos — enters that zone.

Minimum size

The prism symbol is the reference for the margin — always leave at least one symbol of clear space around the logo, and never render the symbol below 16px.

- Primary lockup24px tall on screen · 12mm in print
- Symbol16px tall on screen · 8mm in print

Misuse

## Please don't do these

The logo stays recognisable only if it stays consistent. Six ways to get it wrong — avoid all of them.

Don't recolour

Keep the brand colours. Never swap the palette or tint the mark.

Don't distort

Scale proportionally. Never stretch, squash, or skew.

Don't rotate

The lockup is always level. Never tilt or set it on an angle.

Don't add effects

No shadows, glows, gradients, or outlines on the logo.

Don't crowd it

Respect the clear space. Keep type, edges, and other marks out.

Don't use low contrast

Pick a treatment with enough contrast for the background.

Brand elements

## The pieces that make it feel like Prisma

Beyond the logo, four recurring elements carry the brand. Use them the way we do so partner-made artwork feels native, not borrowed.

The prism ray

The triple-band ray marks where something is happening or where you are — a state, never a static stripe.

Glass prisms

Any shape can be a Prisma. Translucent glass sits in the background, cropped by section edges — never over copy.

Grain

A fine film grain over colour-washed panels — present but never a visible image. Keeps flat surfaces from feeling sterile.

The cube grid

An isometric grid of cubes — the structured, composable feel of the data layer. Move your cursor over it.

Mascot

## Meet Prismo

Prismo is the face of the agent — the chrome head with the prism visor you'll meet on the homepage. Reach for him where the product should feel human, and leave the logo to do the branding.

Four rules for using him

- Prismo is a character, not a logo. He never stands in for the prism mark or the wordmark.
- One expression per surface. Repeated down a page he stops reading as a character and starts reading as wallpaper.
- Match the face to the message — the sad one never carries good news.
- Keep him whole and level. No cropping past the base, no tilting, and never recolour the visor bands.

### Nine expressions

One face per moment. Every expression is a transparent PNG, and all nine ship inside the master download.

Co-branding

## Putting Prisma next to your logo

For integration pages, partner banners, and podcast art, pair the marks with equal weight and honest spacing.

Three rules for a clean pairing

- Give each logo equal optical weight — match by height, not by bounding box.
- Separate the two marks with a thin vertical divider and at least one clear-space unit either side.
- Use the treatment that carries best on the shared background — full colour on light, white on dark.
