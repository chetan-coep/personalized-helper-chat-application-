## What we care about

### Open source

To support the OSS community and help fund the ecosystem around Prisma, we started our Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) Fund in April 2022. Each month Prisma donates $500 to a selected open-source project, nominated by the team, reviewed across the company, and announced on social media.

### Climate change

Prisma supports initiatives that raise awareness about and combat the effects of climate change: we match employee donations to climate charities, offset the carbon footprint of company travel, and commit 1% of earned revenue to fund carbon removal.
