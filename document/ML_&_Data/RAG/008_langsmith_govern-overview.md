# Govern - Docs by LangChain

Source: https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/govern-overview

---

Administer your LangSmith organization: manage users and access control, organize workspaces and applications, and configure policies and compliance. Governance also extends to what agents do at runtime:

[spend and data protection policies](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/llm-gateway-spend-policies)on model calls,

[sandboxed execution](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/sandboxes)for agent code, and

[human approval](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/add-human-in-the-loop)before sensitive actions.
