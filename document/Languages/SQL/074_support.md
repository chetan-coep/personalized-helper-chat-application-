PostgreSQL has a wide variety of community and commercial support options available for users, including:

[Commercial support](https://www.postgresql.org/support/professional_support/) is also available from one of the many companies providing [professional services](https://www.postgresql.org/support/professional_support/)
to the PostgreSQL community. A list of companies that provide [PostgreSQL-specific hosting](https://www.postgresql.org/support/professional_hosting/) is
also available.

There are websites and other resources that may also be extremely useful, some of which are listed below.

**Note that the following resources are not run by the PostgreSQL Project, and the PostgreSQL Code of Conduct may not apply.**

To suggest a resource to be included here, please email [webmaster@postgresql.org](mailto:webmaster@postgresql.org).

Found a bug in PostgreSQL? Please read over our [bug reporting guidelines](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/bug-reporting.html)
and then report it using our [bug reporting form](https://www.postgresql.org/account/submitbug/).

You can see previous bug reports, and track your own on the [pgsql-bugs@lists.postgresql.org](https://www.postgresql.org/list/pgsql-bugs/) mailing
list.

If you believe you have found a security issue, please refer to the
   [security page](https://www.postgresql.org/support/security/) for information on how to
   report it.
